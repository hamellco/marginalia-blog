Marginalia is the personal writing notebook of Griffin D. Hamell — essays and
link commentary on pop culture, technology, and writing.

Set in _Fraunces_ for display, _Newsreader_ for reading, and _Space Grotesk_ for
instrumentation. Built on a small custom static engine — no framework — and
published from plain Markdown.
